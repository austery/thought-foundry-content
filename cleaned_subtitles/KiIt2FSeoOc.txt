Distinguished leaders , guests , and parents who care about children's healthy lives , hello everyone . I am from the Peking University Binhai Experimental School . Just now , the Secretary-General mentioned that our foundation was established eight years ago , and our school is also celebrating its eighth anniversary this year . The school started with just 20 students and has grown to where it is today . It now has 196 teaching classes . With over 8,300 teachers and students , why has a school come such a long way in just eight years and become one of the top quality schools in the region ? I think my approach to education started with first helping children move toward health . As you can see , our students ' high school entrance exam scores have ranked first in our Binhai district for four consecutive years , and our students ' physical health test scores are far above the Tianjin average . In 2024 , Peking University conducted a physical fitness test for students . The national average was 71.3 points , while our school’s students reached 88.3 points , far exceeding the Tianjin city average of 74.3 points . Not only that , the myopia rate of all our students has actually dropped by 4 % in the last three years . At a seminar in Beijing , an expert once told me , " I’ll get angry at anyone who claims that myopia rates can actually go down now . " I said , " Then the two of us can get angry together . " Because our students ' myopia rates have truly , genuinely , and verifiably declined . Furthermore , we are also very outstanding in competitive sports . Currently , our school's primary school girls ' soccer team has been the champion for three consecutive years . Beyond that , our karate , basketball , and volleyball teams have all achieved excellent results in many city-wide and even national competitions . You might be wondering how you managed to improve students ' physical health so significantly . First , we have insisted on having one P.E. class every single day . Both primary and middle school levels have fully achieved this . At the same time , our long recess periods are 40 minutes in the morning and 35 minutes in the afternoon . I have insisted on this for six years . No one demanded that I , as principal , must make the recesses this long ; no one managed us . But we simply insisted on doing it ourselves . In our school , loving sports is not just a requirement from the principal or pressure from teachers ; it has become a conscious habit for the students . We often see children carrying their school bags to school . At our school gate , you will see children walking onto campus carrying basketballs and badminton rackets . You might ask , Principal , why do you place so much importance on physical education ? Is it because of physical education requirements for the high school entrance exam , or is it that I’m only thinking this way because others expect me to ? It is because I genuinely see that the health of modern teenagers is truly worrying ; chubby kids , little kids with glasses , and various psychological problems are currently major challenges troubling parents and schools alike . How can we solve these problems ? Why do these problems arise ? I have given this some thought . Look at these three pictures ; this is what I experienced in my childhood — walking to school , cutting grass and herding cattle , catching fish in ponds , and climbing walls and trees . These three photos below show the reality of modern children’s lives : school during the day , tutoring at night , and riding in cars to get to school . Accompanied by screens . Our children are immersed in this kind of world all day long . From ancient times to the present , we humans have relied on our hands and legs to sustain our lives . Now that we have entered an industrial civilization and an information society , our physical labor is decreasing ; so , how can we ensure our physical health ? Where can we turn for physical health ? Thus , I proposed this viewpoint : the era that least requires physical strength is precisely the era where we need physical education the most . As for schools , everyone knows that emphasizing physical education nowadays faces many hurdles , which I have summarized as " passing five barriers . " The first is the teachers ' capability — how should we build our teaching staff ? At our school , my first priority is to have everyone understand and support my philosophy . I tell everyone that the current students ' physical fitness is concerning . As a principal running a school , I must strive to ensure every student possesses great vitality and resilience . Based on this , I started organizing our physical education staff ; how should we develop it ? It starts with the recruitment process . Our school fights hard to secure autonomy in hiring teachers . As you know , public schools usually hire teachers based on written exams followed by a total score . In this process , candidates are tested on English proficiency , pedagogy , and psychology . But that is not my approach , because I am looking for PE teachers , and what I need is their exceptional ability to teach sports . I need them to have excellent moral character as educators . So , I have two selection criteria : first , your professional expertise must be solid ; second , your moral character must be high . The teacher on the left is named Xiao Lijuan . She is a PE teacher I hired through an exceptional admission process . He didn't have English proficiency certifications , nor could he pass pedagogy or psychology exams , but he was the captain of the Tianjin women's soccer team . I thought to myself that a team captain must be a great soccer player , and her organizational skills and character must be excellent , so I made an exception and hired her . As it turned out , under her leadership , our school's soccer program has thrived ; it is fair to say she created this miracle . The teacher on the right is named Geng ; he is a former national karate champion and a member of the national team . Although he would also struggle with English tests and pedagogy or psychology exams , I hired him as well . In this year's national karate tournament , at the Haikou leg , we won three golds , five silvers , and two bronzes . We achieved first place in the total team score for the U14 group . Just think , if I had followed standard hiring criteria , such a teacher might never have even made it through my door . Secondly , after hiring these teachers , how do you cultivate them ? My philosophy for managing teachers is simple . It consists of twelve characters : first , relationships — once you enter my campus , forming cliques or engaging in cronyism is not allowed . No gift-giving . At the start of this school year , we already had over 600 teachers . With 635 teachers sitting in the auditorium , I asked everyone present , stating that our school’s core philosophy is simple relationships and no gift-giving . I asked , " I'd like to ask everyone here : as graduates from ordinary universities entering our campus , did any of you have to pay a single cent ? " " You can raise your hand . " In reality , absolutely no one would raise their hand . Why does the school advocate for simple relationships ? So where should you put your energy ? Your energy should be focused on your professional work . Only with strong professional competence can you cultivate outstanding students . The third point is to have a broad perspective as a P.E. teacher . You must be able to endure hardship . You can never say , " There's a sports meet tomorrow morning , so I'm putting together the program tonight , Principal — pay me overtime . " That simply doesn't exist in our school . Everyone works wholeheartedly , putting their heart and soul into this collective . Everyone contributes their strength , and this is the school's positive , rich atmosphere that has shaped our internal ecosystem . With these twelve characters as our foundation , internal relationships become very simple and harmonious , and everyone works together toward one goal : to do a great job in sports education . How can we make our students stronger ? That brings us to a key question : what kind of curriculum do you have for students to receive physical education ? Our school has built its own curriculum system . We have a three-tier system : foundational courses , elective courses , and specialized courses . Foundational courses are required for everyone . Elective courses are designed to cultivate personal interests , while specialized courses are for those children who have a talent , for example , if I love playing soccer . For instance , we have an elementary student named Yang Tong who has now made it into the Tianjin elite team . This child underwent training with our PE department and showed exceptional talent in soccer , so our school provided a space for him to pursue his own path . This is what a specialized course is . I would like to focus here on our foundational courses , which every student must take . For example , large-scale exercises are something everyone cares about , yet they are very difficult to do well . Traditional methods like doing calisthenics and running don't attract students . So , how can we get students to voluntarily step onto the field , take initiative , and happily immerse themselves in running and jumping ? We completely redesigned them ourselves . In this regard , we must mention a teacher we hired . He isn't one who passed the typical exams , but his design skills and ability to manage large-scale events are extraordinary . His name is Dai Litong , and he led our PE faculty to independently develop 86 large-scale activity programs . These programs are entirely tailored to students ' endurance , speed , flexibility , coordination , and explosive power , making children want to step onto the field and exercise . This is the middle school program called " Youth , " and this one is for elementary school , called " Sunshine Teenagers . " When our children walk onto the playground and see these activity programs , how could they not love them ? There are various competitions between classes and groups , so once the children are on the field , it’s not me , the principal , forcing them to exercise — they are participating in these activities actively and of their own volition . We don't just have dynamic sports ; we also have student group gymnastics . Look , we have over 3,000 students on the playground all performing these movements together . These are our children . They said our foundation posted a message online . After posting a video of our students , some people claimed it was fake . I tell you , every morning and afternoon , this kind of excitement is happening . Such high-intensity , high-quality physical activities . Just think about it , what if a safety issue occurs ? I want to promote sports , and there isn't a principal who doesn't get a headache from this . I say that to promote sports , safety is the guarantee ; otherwise , the parents alone could get you fired . We can certainly use methods to protect children ; for example , don't go out on cloudy days , and when it rains or snows , close the doors . Wouldn't that keep children very safely inside the classrooms without any accidents ? So , that is one way of thinking . But my other approach is to strengthen education by letting children gain the ability to avoid risks , thereby achieving the goal of safety . This is my philosophy . I say that in promoting sports , we are honing students ' ability to avoid danger . So , how do I train the students ' capabilities ? When others finish school , they lock the doors , but when I finish school , I make an announcement over the loudspeakers for all teachers and students to go to the playground . Tianjin TV found out about this . They immediately brought their cameras to our school . On the first day of school , they broadcast across the city that Beijing has a very strange school where students have snowball fights on the first day . In fact , I just want the children to practice this kind of self-protection ability . One day it snowed heavily , and we have a platform on the second floor with many exits . Our logistics director told me , " Principal , it's snowing today . Let's lock the doors on the second-floor platform . What if the children go out and fall or get hurt ? " I just said one thing : " Listen to me , open all the doors . " As a result , our director actually opened them all . I think of it this way : when our children go out the door , they definitely want to run . When they are about to fall , in that split second , their brain must be thinking one thing : " How can I not fall ? " If they think like that today and tomorrow , just think , hasn't their ability to resist risks and falls been formed ? Isn't that much more valuable than the kind of safety formed by locking them up ? When teachers on duty see children running , they say , " Hurry , don't run , don't fall . " But I don't think that way . I say , " If you want to run , run ; if you're going to fall , you fall . " I say , " If they fall today , they will know to be careful when walking tomorrow . " Just think , won't their future life also have storms and hardships ? Our children can't even walk on level ground . If they can't even endure the weather in wind and snow , how will they handle life in the future ? Our students , through such day-after-day , year-after-year training , have forged their bodies and developed strong willpower . So , think about it : with such good character , such will , and such determination in their studies , how could they not excel ? Then there is another question everyone might ask : what if a child falls down ? Here is what I think . We have 7,735 children . If one child falls down . And 7,734 children benefit , I think it's worth it . Just like field trips ; many institutions stopped them for fear of accidents , but I have insisted until now that if an accident happens , I will take responsibility . No problem . But when children truly go out , they can benefit from it ; that is my safety philosophy . I say that with such emphasis on sports , parents have a bigger concern : what about the children's grades ? If you focus on sports every day , letting kids run and jump on the playground , what if they don't test well ? Would parents send their children to your school if they didn't test well ? I say that is impossible . I think the first thing is to grasp classroom efficiency ; everyone here can walk into any classroom , listen to a 45 - minute or 40 - minute lesson , and see how much class time is wasted . Some irresponsible teachers walk in with their books and ramble for ten or twenty minutes without ever getting to the point . I say the problem today isn't a lack of class time ; some teachers truly don't use the time well . In light of this , I lead our principals , vice-principals , and all our management staff to dive straight into the classrooms to observe lessons every day . We guide teachers on how to help students gain more in the same amount of time , with the goal of improving classroom efficiency . We free up time so children can get onto the field ; I personally attend at least 200 classes a year . Think about it , I make a full round of our 196 classes . Spending at least an hour and a half observing each class . I am very busy , but I still go into the classrooms because the point is to grasp classroom efficiency . Besides focusing on classroom efficiency , I believe our focus on sports actually has a very positive effect on grades as well . I say that physical exercise not only improves physical fitness and regulates students ' mental states , but also promotes cognitive development , tempers character , cultivates a spirit of cooperation , and even treats certain psychological ailments . It plays a very positive role in many aspects . For instance , we had a student surnamed Xie , who was hyperactive throughout his kindergarten and elementary school years . I often saw him making all sorts of sounds and movements in the classroom . Other students couldn't attend class , and parents wanted him to transfer . But for a child like this , our teachers didn't give up ; they led him onto the soccer field to run and jump with the others . In kindergarten and first grade , he relied on medication daily to stabilize his emotions . However , once he stepped onto the soccer field , he completely transformed , immersing his mind and body in the running and competing . His hyperactivity gradually faded away . He was no longer hyperactive . He also stabilized . By the second grade , he stopped taking his medication . Seeing he was no longer hyperactive , teachers advised him , " Why don't you focus on your studies and play a little less soccer ? " Upon hearing this , his parents told the teacher , " He can skip meals , he can skip school , but he cannot stop playing soccer . Soccer has given my son a second life . " This very child has now joined the Tianjin elite team , and his academic performance is excellent . Think about it , hasn't soccer truly changed this child's destiny ? There is another example . When this girl first entered our school , she had over 1,000 degrees of astigmatism ; she couldn't see the eye chart at all , only the " E " on the very top row . She was a timid girl who wouldn't dare move , yet she joined our karate training team . Our teachers guided her step-by-step , training her courage and temperament . Later , she became more and more confident and brave . She eventually won the national karate championship and became a national second-tier athlete . When she entered the first year of junior high , in a district-wide exam , she actually ranked first in the Binhai New Area in her academic subjects . Think about it , does a well-developed physique necessarily mean a simple mind ? That is not the case ; a well-developed physique can absolutely make a child's mind sharper . It's not just individual cases ; for years , our school's middle-to-high school entrance exam results have consistently ranked first among the 41 public schools in the Binhai New Area . Think about it , with a school like this , what parent wouldn't want to send their child here ? Our student body has grown from just 20 students to over 8,300 students and teachers today . So , I’d say , this kind of support doesn't just come from me pushing teachers and students ; there’s a key factor : you have to win the parents ' support for athletics . If parents hold you back , I’m telling you , you will never get anything done . If you want to win them over and get their support , you must first do your own job well and let the facts speak for themselves , like our test scores being the best in the district . For example , in various competitions , our students ' physical health scores are excellent . Just think , if a school has both great results and high standards , who wouldn't be on board ? So , as a principal , if you want social satisfaction , you must do your job well , ensuring teachers treat every child with care and responsibility , then the parents . Will surely be pleased . That’s the first point . As for the second point , I think as a principal , you need to communicate with parents sincerely , effectively , and seriously , to achieve what ? Reasoning with them , you must explain to parents why I’m prioritizing athletics . At every annual parents ' meeting , I always give the first lesson to the new parents . Since our auditorium only holds 400 people this year , I had to host six sessions , each lasting over two hours . And what do I tell the parents ? I tell them that once your child is in our school , there’s a chance they might get hurt or break a bone someday . If they do get hurt , please don’t call the 12345 hotline . The biggest tragedy in education today is that parents call 12345 at the drop of a hat . Look , in a school this big , not every teacher is perfect ; it’s impossible for everyone to be equally excellent . I , Principal Zhu , might be considered fairly good , but I know I still have plenty of flaws . So , if your child walks into school and happens to bump , scrape , or fall , I believe it's normal . You can use proper channels to report it , like calling me : " Principal Zhu , I noticed an issue with one of your teachers . " But please , don’t call 12345 . If you call 12345 , I can tell you the process : first , the Education Bureau forwards the complaint to me ; second , I have to find someone , investigate , and write up a report . I’ll have to write : " Dear parent , thank you for your feedback and suggestions regarding our work . " I’m telling you , the truth is , I’m absolutely furious inside when I do that . I told them , if you have that kind of time , you'd be better off calling me , letting me quietly slip into some classrooms to observe the students and listen to the teachers . So , even though I say that I have very strict requirements for students and they train very hard , every time we hold a parent meeting , when I mention how demanding I am of the children , the parents always give me a long round of applause . Therefore , I say , parents are reasonable , and parents are also capable of understanding . The key is , as a principal , have you shared your thoughts , your intentions , and your heart with the parents ? Are you truly treating every student with care ? This is the most crucial point in determining whether parents will understand and support you . In recent years , the banners that parents have sent to our school have reached over 1,000 . They cover the walls . Currently , we have a banner hall , but it is now completely full ; we can’t fit any more , so I had them all stacked up , neatly registered and stacked away . So I think , what does the parents ' support for our school stem from ? It stems from your profound understanding of education , from using your heart to truly treat every child , and from truly grasping what the future society needs in terms of talent . As principals and teachers engaged in the cause of education . I say we should consider issues more from the perspective of the country , the nation , and the students . At this year's parent meeting , I told the parents that we are a 12 - year integrated school . If you allow a child to spend 12 years in my school , from first grade on , it is equivalent to entrusting your child's life to me ; I have no reason not to treat your child and that trust seriously . So I say , as principals , what should we do ? We should truly humble ourselves , take on the responsibility , and work with heart , firmly believing that if you do it earnestly and with care , it will surely lead to a better , broader , and more ...
