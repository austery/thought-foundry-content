The mechanics simplified are as follows. Essentially Nvidia makes the chips that power AI. The hyperscalers Microsoft, Amazon, Google Meta buy these chips to build data centers that they need. They pay for the chips using capital raised against projected future AI revenue. That AI revenue depends on customers and end users like us adopting AI products at scale. NVIDIA books revenue, the hyperscalers book capital expenditure. Everyone's numbers look big, but the actual enduser demand, I'm talking real people here, real businesses paying real money for AI products that generate real returns, hasn't really scaled to fill the infrastructure being built for it.
